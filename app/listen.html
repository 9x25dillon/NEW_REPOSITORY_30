One body per line: a name, then its ecliptic longitude in degrees. Compute it wherever you like — this page sounds a chart, it does not cast one.

This is music. It may well be pleasant, which is a real thing for music to be and needs no mechanism beyond the ordinary one. It is not a dose or a treatment, and it is not the process the bench models — that runs at a million hertz and upward inside a channel, where the wavelength in water is under a millimetre. Here the wavelength is metres. The two share no code, and a test enforces it.
