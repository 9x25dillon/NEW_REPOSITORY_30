Before you begin

Photosensitivity notice: this page renders slowly animated visuals. All visual modulation is capped at 2.5 Hz, below the 3–30 Hz range associated with photosensitive risk — but if you are sensitive to flashing or pulsing imagery, or experience discomfort, close the page.

Audio notice: tones start at a low level. Set your system volume low before starting, especially with headphones.

What this is: an aesthetic, generative audiovisual instrument seeded from chart geometry. It is not a medical device and makes no diagnostic, therapeutic, or health claims of any kind.

Privacy: everything runs locally in this page. No network requests are made. Chart data stays in memory only and is never written to browser storage, logs, or exports unless you explicitly opt in.
